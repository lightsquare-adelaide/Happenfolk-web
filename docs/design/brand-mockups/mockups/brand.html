Rules

  • The mark is always one colour: ink or white. Never pink alone.
  • Accent appears on interactive elements, focus rings and trending rank markers only.
  • Never place the mark or the accent on top of artwork.
  • The acid highlight appears once per screen, on rank #1, in dark theme.
  • Write the name as one word: Happenfolk. Caps only in the wordmark.
  • Keep the copy flat: “3 spots left”, not “Hurry, almost full!”
