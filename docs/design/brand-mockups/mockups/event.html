About this event

An evening of live sets on the Semaphore foreshore, timed to the low tide. Four local acts play about 30 minutes each, and the last set finishes at 10:15 pm.

Bring something to sit on. There is a covered area if the weather turns, and the kiosk serves food until 9 pm.

All ages. The organisers will add photos from the night to this page.
